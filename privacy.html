YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy information

BN Private Beach – Jinja · Buyala–Budondo, Jinja, Uganda

When you request a reservation, ask a question, or leave a review on our website, we collect the details you choose to provide so our team can assist you.

What we collect

Booking requests may include your name, telephone number, email, dates, number of guests, selected accommodation, and special requests. Enquiries contain your contact details and message. Reviews contain a display name, star rating, and review text.

Why we use it

We use this information to reply to enquiries, assess booking availability, manage reservations, moderate guest reviews, and improve our services. We do not publish booking details, phone numbers, or email addresses. Approved reviews display only the guest's chosen display name, rating, and review text.

Payments

Secure online checkout, once activated, is provided by an independent payment provider. Guests approve Mobile Money payments through their network's secure process. BN Private Beach never asks you to enter your Mobile Money PIN into our website or share a PIN with our staff.

Access and retention

Only authorised staff should have access to the private booking and enquiry dashboard. We retain records for as long as reasonably needed for reservations, service improvement, and applicable obligations, then review them for deletion.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you've submitted or to request correction or deletion, subject to applicable retention requirements. Please contact us on 0780 350 151 or 0704 244 566.

Before publishing: BN Private Beach management should confirm this notice, its actual data-retention process, and any legal obligations in Uganda.
