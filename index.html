GOOD TO KNOW

A few things before you go.

Is breakfast included in the stay?

Yes. Our quoted cottage rates include bed and breakfast unless your personalised package specifies otherwise.

Can we book as a corporate group or family?

Absolutely. We host groups, retreats, family getaways and special occasions. Share the dates and expected group size and we'll propose a package.

Can I book and pay online?

You can submit a booking request here. Our team confirms availability first; secure online Mobile Money checkout becomes available after your booking is approved and the payment service is activated. Never enter your PIN on this site.

How do I check my booking?

Use the booking reference you receive after sending your request, together with the phone number you submitted. You can check it below or speak to us on WhatsApp.
